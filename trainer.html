Running count is not reset each hand — it is the total of every card dealt since the shoe was last shuffled, cards from earlier hands included. Add up the Hi-Lo value of every card you can see on the table right now (both your cards and the dealer's visible upcard — the hole card doesn't count yet): 2–6 = +1, 7–9 = 0, 10/J/Q/K/A = −1.

True count = running count ÷ decks remaining. Decks remaining is shown just below this box in the shoe line — divide your running count by that number and round to the nearest whole number (the app shows the exact decimal in feedback for reference, but only grades the rounded value).

If this is the first hand after a shuffle, the running count starts at 0, so it's just the total of the cards you see now.

Does the count change after the hand plays out? Yes. The count you enter here is only for the cards on the table at this moment (before any decision). Once you act, any extra cards dealt to finish the hand — your hits, the dealer's hits — are new cards too, and they're shown on the table after the hand resolves. Fold their values into your running count before the next hand's prompt.
